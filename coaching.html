Coaching for executive directors and directors of development

The Four Receipts

Coaching that leaves you with receipts: commitments anyone can check, persistent problems resolved or handed back, and a record of what you kept.

Not superman. Leverage. Coaching starts with you as the point where your organization, and the profession, can change.

What you leave with

Skills you can show, not just claim

  • Making checkable commitments: a standard, a deadline, an owner and evidence.
  • Tracing a failure to its cause: what produced it, not who.
  • Managing up and across: handing back, in writing, what belongs to the board or another department.
  • Building systems that outlast you: a report, a standard, a handoff that no longer depends on memory.

After six months, your portfolio holds every kept promise and its evidence: interview stories with proof attached.

A persistent challenge, finally ended

Bring the problem that keeps coming back. Within six months, it reaches one of two endings:

  • Resolved, with evidence that it was kept.
  • Traced and handed back: the assumption behind it named, and the problem sent to the role that owns it.

Both count. The second is often the bigger win: you stop carrying what was never yours to fix.

How it works

Getting started

  1. A conversation. We decide whether coaching fits.
  2. Enrol. Agreement and invoice; you sign in to your ledger.
  3. Three questions before we meet, about your story and your promises.
  4. First session. The story first; we set your goals.

Every two weeks

  1. MadeA promise worth paying for
  2. OwnedMine, not the institution’s
  3. KeptAction, not just planning
  4. ProvedEvidence it was kept

You work the cycle in your ledger; we meet at the end of each cycle, starting from the record, not from memory. Goals are reviewed at three and six months.

Your Four Receipts Ledger

A private web app for your promises, every two weeks. It keeps your history, shows what keeps slipping and whose obstacle it is, tracks your goals, and builds your portfolio of kept promises.

Private to you and the coach you invite. Your coach can read but never change it, and you can remove access at any time.
