Why both

The visible receipts and the invisible ones are kept in different parts of the organization. Donors only ever see the total.

Every gift sets three parties in relation: the donor, the organization, and the people the gift is finally for. A receipt is the one thing that touches all three at once. The donor sees it. The organization has to produce it. The beneficiary is the reason it exists.

Neither role can keep that promise alone. The development office makes most of the visible promises. The rest of the organization keeps most of the invisible ones. Trust is what builds up when both halves hold, one kept promise at a time.
