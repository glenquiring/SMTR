Made

What promises are active this cycle, and what will keeping them cost?

A promise without evidence is aspirational, not verifiable. Write each one so someone else could check it.

2

Owned

Sort each promise. Is it mine to carry, the institution’s, or shared? Hand back what is not mine.

3

Kept

What did you finish? What slipped, and by when will it be done?

4

Proved

What shows it was kept? If it was not, what produced the slip, and whose obstacle was it?

The standing question

Who have I turned into a character this cycle?

Name the role you have given them in a few words. Then: what would they say my decisions look like from their side of the desk?

        For the coach: running the check-in
        
            
          
          • Read the receipt before the session. Start from the record, not from “how’s it going?”
            • 
          
          • Understand, then challenge. Hear the story behind a slip before pressing on it.
            • 
          
          • Ask what produced the slip, not who caused it. That includes when the “who” is the leader.
            • 
          
          • Watch the Owned sort. Is the leader carrying the institution’s promises, or handing back their own?
            • 
          
          • Use the History tab every few cycles: repeat slips and the obstacle tally show the pattern.
            • 
          
          • Take the standing question seriously. Ask what the person they named would see.
            • 
          
          • Close with the next cycle’s promises, each written so you could check it.
